# Chargeback Monitoring Programs and Account Consequences

Payment networks (Visa and Mastercard) maintain dispute monitoring programs (such as Visa Dispute Monitoring Program - VDMP and Mastercard Excessive Dispute Program). These programs track a merchant's monthly dispute-to-transaction ratio.

Crossing threshold levels (typically 0.9% dispute ratio and 100 disputes/month for Visa standard tier) results in severe penalties:
- Substantial monthly network fines ($50+ per dispute).
- Increased scrutiny, higher processing reserves, or mandatory remediation plans.
- Risk of account termination or merchant match listing if unaddressed.
Merchants approaching thresholds must immediately deploy fraud prevention measures (Radar rules, mandatory 3DS, early fraud warnings/Verifi/Ethoca alerts) and refund high-risk orders before they turn into disputes.
