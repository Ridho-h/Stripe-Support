# Volume Pricing and Sales Routing for High-Scale Accounts

Stripe offers standard pay-as-you-go pricing for transactions, billing, and ancillary services. For rapidly growing businesses and enterprise accounts exceeding standard processing thresholds (typically $100k+ in monthly processing volume), customized volume-based pricing is available through Stripe's Enterprise Sales team.

Custom pricing arrangements can encompass discounted interchange-plus rates, bundled international acquiring discounts, tailored chargeback support, and dedicated technical account management. Account holders interested in evaluating custom pricing contracts should be routed to a sales representative or account executive with details regarding their annualized volume, average ticket size, and geographic mix.
